Descriptions

One line

Apparent is a calm daily loop for parents and kids on their own devices: you set the day, your child sees one clear next step, you approve what needs a look, and rewards update on both screens.

A paragraph

Apparent is a calm daily loop for parents and kids on their own devices.

You set the day. Your child sees one clear next step. You approve what needs a look. Rewards update on both screens.

We built it on published research, which meant leaving most things out. No streaks. No reminders sent to your child. No algorithm deciding what a task is worth. You set the stars, you choose the rewards, and the app keeps the math honest.
