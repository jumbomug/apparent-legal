Apparent Family — Age Suitability

Updated: July 19, 2026

Apparent Family is a chores-and-rewards app for families, rated 4+. A parent or caretaker sets up and manages everything; a child uses a simple, game-like task list on their own device.

Suitable for all ages

  • No objectionable content. The app contains only the tasks, rewards, and star values a family creates for itself — no violence, sexual content, profanity, gambling, horror themes, or mature content of any kind.
  • No advertising of any kind, first- or third-party.
  • No social features. No social media, no feeds, no user-to-user chat, and no content shared outside a family's own private account.
  • No unrestricted web access. The app contains no browser.
  • No purchases. The app contains no in-app purchases, loot boxes, or paid promotions.

Parent-managed by design

  • A parent creates the account, enters all household and child details, and controls every task and reward.
  • Children do not create accounts, enter personal information, or communicate with anyone outside their family. The only child input is completing tasks and an optional short note to their own parent when submitting a task for review.
  • We collect an age group (e.g. "elementary"), never a birthdate. The camera is used only to scan a parent-provided invite QR code; no photos are taken or stored.
  • No analytics, tracking, or data sharing — see the Privacy Policy for details.
