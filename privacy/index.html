Apparent Family — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026

Apparent Family (“Apparent,” “we,” “us”) is a family app that helps a parent or caretaker set a child’s daily tasks, and helps the child complete them and earn rewards, across their two devices. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We built Apparent to collect as little as possible.

Who provides the information

A parent or caretaker creates the account and enters the household and child details. A child uses a linked device that a parent connects with a short-lived invite code; the child does not create their own account and does not enter personal identifying information. Apparent is a parent-managed tool, not a service children sign up for on their own.

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Parent account: your email address, and — if you use Sign in with Apple — the name and email (or Apple private-relay email) Apple provides. Used to create and secure your account.
  • Household setup: a household name you choose and the number of parents/caretakers.
  • Child profile: a display name you choose for the child and an age group (for example “elementary”) — never an exact birthdate.
  • Tasks and rewards: the responsibilities, routines, star values, rewards, and completion history you create and track, including any short note a child attaches when sending a task for a parent’s review.
  • Device connection: short-lived, single-use invite codes (stored only as a hash) used to connect a child’s device to your family.
  • Push token: a notification token so we can tell a parent when a child’s task is waiting for review.

We do not collect precise location, financial information, health data, contacts, browsing or search history, or biometric data. The camera is used only to scan a parent-provided invite QR code — no photos are taken or stored.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: create and sync your family’s tasks and rewards across the parent and child devices.
  • To send a parent a push notification when a child submits a task for review. Notifications contain only generic text (e.g. “A task is ready for review”) and an identifier the app resolves after you open it — never a child’s name or task details.
  • To secure accounts and prevent abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

Tracking and analytics

Apparent contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs. We do not track you or your family across other apps or websites.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the infrastructure providers that run the app, and only as needed to operate it:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and secure sync.
  • Expo (push notifications) — delivers the review-request notifications described above.
  • Apple — provides Sign in with Apple when you choose it.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms; they are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not share family data with anyone else.

Children’s privacy

Apparent is designed for parents to manage on behalf of their children:

  • A parent creates the account and provides the child’s display name and age group. Children do not create accounts or submit personal information directly.
  • We minimize children’s data by design — an age group, not a birthdate, and no photos, location, or contact data.
  • We do not use children’s data for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

If you believe a child’s information has been provided to us in a way you did not intend, contact us at nicholas.ryan.larsen@gmail.com and we will delete it.

How your data is protected

  • Encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • Access is enforced on our servers with per-family, per-role rules — a device can only reach the data its family and role allow.
  • The on-device cache is encrypted.

Retention and deletion

You control your data. From the app’s Settings → Delete account, you can permanently delete your account and all associated family data — children, tasks, rewards, history, and connected-device access — at any time. This is immediate and irreversible.

Your choices

  • Use Sign in with Apple to share less (Apple can hide your real email).
  • Decline or disable notifications (the app still works; you’ll see review requests when you open it).
  • Delete your account and data at any time, as above.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
