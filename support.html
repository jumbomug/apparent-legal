The first day

Getting started

Set up the parent device first. The child's first screen then has a day to show.

  1. Create the parent account

    On your iPhone or iPad, choose Set up as parent, then sign up with Sign in with Apple or an email and password.

  2. Set up the household

    Add a child, choose a few clear tasks, and make the rewards that fit your home.

  3. Connect the child's device

    From the child's page in Family, connect their iPhone or iPad with a QR code or a typed code.

  4. Start the daily loop

    Your child finishes tasks, you review only the ones you chose to check, and both devices show the result.

Two devices

Connect a child's device

Children never create accounts or passwords. Before the device connects, we ask your permission and show you what it sends.

On your device

  1. Open Family, then choose the child.
  2. Select Link a device.
  3. Keep the QR code and the code on screen.

On the child's iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Apparent and choose I am a child.
  2. Scan the QR code or type the code.
  3. Let the first sync finish.
If the code doesn't work

A code lasts 30 minutes and works once. Make a new one on your device and try again. Camera access is optional; typing the code works the same way.

Share the day

Invite another parent or guardian

Two parents, a grandparent, or another trusted adult can run the same family from their own devices, and none of them is ever a paid seat. Permission for a child's device is different: only the child's parent or legal guardian may give it, and we email every adult in the family to confirm.

  1. Send the invite

    Open Family, then Invite a grown-up, then Invite a guardian.

  2. They join

    They install Apparent, choose Set up as parent (or I already have an account), then Join a family and enter the code.

Everyone sees the same family

A guardian can review tasks, answer help requests, and run the day. Handling something on one device clears it for everyone.

The daily loop

Tasks, reviews, and excused days

Your child finishes most tasks with Hold to finish. A task you chose to check comes to you with an optional note, and each review has three choices.

Approve

The task is done and its stars land in your child's balance.

Try again

Send it back with an optional note. Your child reads “Your parent asked you to try this one again.” and sends it again when ready.

Excuse today

It counts toward today's Daily Unlock, earns no stars, and is never shown as done.

Undo

Tapped too fast? Undo a decision within 60 seconds.

If your child asks for help

Your child taps the Help button, then Ask for help. A card appears on every grown-up's Today, and Got it clears it for everyone. Asking for help never affects stars or rewards.

For parents only

Notifications

Apparent can tell you when a task is ready for review, a reward is waiting for your OK, a Daily Unlock was earned, or your child asked for help.

  • Choose Turn on notifications when Apparent offers it, then allow the iOS permission.
  • Every notification is titled Apparent Family and leaves out names and task details. Open the app to see who and what.
  • Your child's device never receives notifications.
Turned them off by mistake?

Open iOS Settings, then Notifications, then Apparent Family.

Saved views

Without a connection

The day as it was last saved stays on screen, so a lost connection doesn't turn Today into an empty page.

Still there

Tasks, progress, rewards and grades from the last sync.

Needs a connection

Finishing a task, approving one, changing the day, and claiming a reward.

Reconnect and try again

Apparent doesn't queue changes for later. If a task can't be saved, your child sees “That task couldn't be saved. Hold to try again.”

Optional, uses Apple's Screen Time

Screen Time and the Daily Unlock

A Daily Unlock can hold the apps you choose on your child's device until today's tasks are handled. The hold lifts for the rest of the day and comes back at midnight.

  • You set it up on your child's device, behind a parent check. The apps you choose never leave that device.
  • With your child's Apple ID in your Family Sharing group, your child can't switch it off without you. Without Family Sharing, whoever holds the device can turn it off in Settings.
  • If the app can't tell where things stand, it leaves the hold as it was. A finished task needs a connection to count.
  • Skip it any time: a Daily Unlock still works as a family agreement.
Use Apple's Downtime?

Downtime can lock Apparent too. Add Apparent to Always Allowed on your child's device; the app walks you through it.

Kept in their place

Grades

You can note a short grade for each subject. Your child sees it on a quiet card, along with when it was updated.

Apparent doesn't connect to a school, doesn't judge a grade, and never uses grades for tasks, progress, stars, rewards, or unlocks.

For every child

Accessibility

  • Hold to finish works with VoiceOver: double-tap to finish, with no hold needed.
  • Text follows your Dynamic Type size. With large text or VoiceOver, Today drops the sky and Finn, as Quiet look does.
  • Quiet look is a switch on your child's Pick your color page, for a child who wants less on the screen.
  • Reduce Motion stills the sky, Finn and every celebration.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG AA contrast in light and dark.

Something hard to use? Tell us at support@apparentfamily.com.

Your data, your call

Account, permission, and deletion

Reset a password

  1. On parent sign-in, choose Forgot password?
  2. Enter your email and send the reset link.
  3. Open the link and set a new password.

For email accounts. Sign in with Apple uses your Apple ID. The confirmation doesn't reveal whether an account exists for that address.

Withdraw permission for a child

  1. Open Family, choose the child, then Edit child.
  2. Choose Withdraw permission.

The child's device disconnects. Their tasks and history stay, so you can connect again later.

Delete a child

  1. Open Family and choose the child.
  2. At the bottom, choose Delete name and their information.
  3. Choose Delete permanently.

This removes everything we hold about that child and disconnects their device. It can't be undone.

Delete your account

  1. Open Settings, then Delete account.
  2. Read what will be deleted.
  3. Type DELETE, then choose Permanently delete.

The screen explains what your role in the family lets you remove. Signing out doesn't delete anything.

Deleted data can stay in routine database backups until they rotate, and a record of the permission you gave, with your child's details removed, is kept for three years after the child is deleted.

The privacy policy lists what is kept afterward and for how long. You can also ask us at privacy@apparentfamily.com.
