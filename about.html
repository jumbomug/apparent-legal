The story

Why it exists

Apparent started as a fix for one family's mornings. Chore charts worked for two weeks. Checklists worked for one. Then the household ran on repeated requests again.

So one parent built the loop the family needed: set the day once, let the kid see what's next, approve only what needed a look, and keep the rewards honest. Along the way came the published research on rewards and motivation, and most of what it said was “build less.” Apparent is what's left.

The company

Who makes it

Apparent Family is made and operated by Bear Audio LLC, a one-person company in Colorado.

It runs on two devices, keeps the child's side calm, and collects only what the day needs. The privacy policy says exactly what that is.

The character

Finn

Finn keeps a child company through the day: calm before energetic, capable before cute, and never the one handing out stars.

Finn, the character in Apparent Family, was drawn by Zac Schmidt. Zac created all of Apparent's art, and designed much of how it looks and moves: the sky over the hills, the Hold to finish button and the Daily Unlock moment.

Each child picks their own color, and Finn stays Finn.
